Questions

Before you start.

Do I need bloodwork to start?

No. Your plan is built from your intake. Labs are optional, and when you run them your plan updates to match.

Will I be put on peptides?

Only if they fit your plan, never as the first answer. If one makes sense, you get clear dosing, monitoring and an exit plan. Prescription options go through independent licensed clinicians.

What does the $297 cover, and is there a contract?

Your plan, review call, training app, wearable tracking, weekly calls and written check-ins, and plan updates. It's month-to-month. Labs, peptides and prescriptions are separate.

Do I need a wearable, and where can I do this from?

Most popular wearables connect, and you can start without one. Everything runs online, anywhere in the U.S.

How soon will I notice something?

Energy and sleep usually move first, often within the first month. Body composition typically takes 8 to 12 weeks.
